# Word navigation and playback

The listening pair chips select a pair and play its L word followed by its N
word with a 350ms gap. This is a preview, not an exam: it adds no answers or
scores. Tapping the selected pair again after it finishes replays it without
erasing answers. Locked pairs remain locked. An explicit Stop example button
cancels the preview; other audio actions wait until it ends.

Practice Previous/Next moves through unlocked words of the selected sound,
including wraparound: light → low → lead, or night → no → need. The L/N controls
still switch to the matching partner. The score card's Next follows the same
rule.

The studio button remains busy until the complete two-repeat recording ends,
not merely until the media play request succeeds. Rapid repeat taps do not
restart, overlap or queue another model. Recording is disabled during the model
to prevent recording the example. Changing word, sound, practice language or
tab cancels it; hiding the app also stops it. Failures and a bounded playback
timeout restore the control with a localized error. Speech-synthesis fallback
likewise repeats the word twice and waits for its end.

Interface language remains independent from practice language. This does not
change scoring, microphone permissions, saved data, purchase entitlements or
the submitted Mac binary. Shared source changes reach installed native apps
only through a new store/test build; a PWA deployment does not replace them.
