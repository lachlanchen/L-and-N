### Full description — submitted for review

Do light and night sound too similar? L & N helps you practise hearing and saying the difference in English, Mandarin and Cantonese.

LISTEN FIRST

Choose a word pair and a round of 3, 5 or 7 words. Tap what you heard in order, then check each answer. Replay the pair when you want to hear the contrast again.

SAY ONE WORD

Listen to the model, record your attempt and see what the recognizer heard. Feedback explains the sound cues behind the result rather than leaving you with a bare score. If online recognition cannot identify a word, the app shows your waveform without guessing a pronunciation score.

REPLAY YOUR TAKES

Hear a saved recording on its own or after the model. Practice history stays on your device, subject to available storage. Clearing app data removes saved takes; there is no cloud recording backup.

SEE HOW THE SOUND WORKS

Explore a 3D cutaway of the tongue and airflow. Switch between English, Mandarin and Cantonese exercises, with a separate choice of English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese or Cantonese for the interface. Audio examples use synthetic voices.

No account, ads or advertising trackers. Android word recognition needs internet access and your explicit permission. A new take is sent over HTTPS for transcription, then discarded from the service. Listening and lessons remain available without online recognition.

Start with free pairs in each language. An optional one-time in-app purchase unlocks the rest of the curriculum.

L & N is a practice tool, not a clinical assessment. Its feedback and mouth model are learning aids, not a diagnosis or a measurement of your mouth.
