### Full description

Hear the difference. Feel the articulation. Say one word at a time.

L & N is a focused pronunciation coach for English, Mandarin, and Cantonese. Each short drill shows the target sound inside a word, plays a bundled studio model, and gives a precise tongue and airflow cue before you record.

EXPLAINABLE PRACTICE

• Direct L/N switching with matched word pairs
• English minimal pairs plus original 普通话 and 廣東話 exercises
• Live waveform and onset-spectrum views
• Feedback that separates recording quality, word recognition, nasal evidence, duration, and tone shape
• Cautious personal calibration from repeated clear attempts

LEARN WITH A 3D CUTAWAY

Explore the tongue tip, alveolar ridge, soft palate, and airflow. The L view shows lateral airflow with the nose closed. The N view shows a broad oral seal and lowered soft palate for nasal airflow. It is a teaching model—not a scan of your mouth.

YOUR LANGUAGES, KEPT SEPARATE

Choose English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, or Cantonese for the interface without changing the English, 普通话, or 廣東話 exercise you are practising.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No account, ads, or trackers. Recent attempt summaries and preferences stay on your device. Raw recordings are not retained by L & N.

L & N is an educational tool and not a medical diagnosis or substitute for a qualified speech-language pathologist.
