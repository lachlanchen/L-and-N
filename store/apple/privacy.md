# App Store privacy declaration draft

- Tracking: No
- Data linked to the user: None
- Data not linked to the user: None
- Privacy nutrition label: Data Not Collected

Reasoning: the developer does not receive native-app recordings, scores, progress, identifiers, or analytics. Practice summaries remain locally on the device. A speech request may be handled by the operating system’s speech service; audio is not retained by L & N. Reconfirm this declaration against the exact submitted binary and App Store Connect wording before submission.
