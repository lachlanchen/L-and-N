### Description

L & N turns one easily confused sound contrast into a calm four-minute practice loop.

Choose English, 普通话, or 廣東話. Hear a clear model, see the target inside the word, record one short attempt, and get a score that explains the evidence instead of hiding it behind a single number.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Practise English minimal pairs plus original Mandarin and Cantonese exercises
• Switch directly between the paired L and N sounds
• Inspect the waveform and onset spectrum for silence, timing, and recording quality
• Explore an interactive 3D cutaway of tongue contact, the alveolar ridge, the soft palate, and oral or nasal airflow
• Keep recent progress and cautious personal calibration on your device
• Use a compact listen-and-identify drill on Apple Watch

The interface language is independent from the language you practise. Interface options include English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and Cantonese.

L & N is an educational coaching tool. Its score is not a medical diagnosis, and the 3D model is an explanatory target—not a scan or measurement of your mouth.

No account. No ads. No tracking.

## App Review notes

No account or login is required. To test scoring, select a practice language, tap Record, allow microphone and speech-recognition access, say the displayed word, and stop. If the simulator has no microphone input, all navigation, bundled model audio, the 3D Learn view, and the watch identification drill remain testable.

The native app invokes the operating system speech-recognition service and does not send audio to an L & N server. The privacy policy separately describes the hosted PWA fallback; that browser-only route is not used by the native binary.

The watchOS app is embedded as a companion with bundle ID `art.lazying.landn.watchkitapp` and provides a listen-and-identify drill without microphone access.
